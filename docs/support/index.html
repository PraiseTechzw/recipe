Support

Questions, feedback, or something not working as expected?

Contact the team

Email praisetechzw@gmail.com with the app version, device model, Android/iOS version and a short description of the issue. Please do not include your password, private recipe data or other sensitive information.

For bugs and feature requests, open an issue on the GitHub issue tracker. Avoid posting personal information in public issues.

Account and data requests

The app currently does not offer in-app account deletion or data export. Contact praisetechzw@gmail.com for account or privacy requests. See the Privacy Policy for more information.

App updates

When a new Google Play release is announced through the project's GitHub release process, supported app versions check periodically and can open the app's Google Play listing. You can also check for updates directly in Google Play.
