Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how Zimbabwe Traditional Recipes handles information when you use the app. The app is operated by Praise Masunga (PraiseTechzw), Zimbabwe. Contact: praisetechzw@gmail.com.

Information the app handles

Depending on the features you use, the app may handle your email address and display name; profile details and avatar; recipes and images you publish; favourites, pantry and shopping-list items; cooking progress, achievements and leaderboard information; app language, theme and notification settings; recipe views and category interactions used to personalize recommendations; and notification permission or a push token on supported devices.

If you use AI Chef, selected ingredient images and the ingredients, pantry items and preferences you provide (which may include dietary restrictions, allergies, time limits and serving sizes) are sent for AI processing.

Where information is stored and shared

Some app state is stored on your device using local storage. When configured and connected, Supabase provides authentication, database, image storage and synchronization. Private app-state records are protected by user-specific database policies. Public recipe and avatar images, published recipes, profiles and leaderboard data may be visible to other people.

AI requests go through a Supabase Edge Function to OpenRouter and may be routed to third-party model providers. The OpenRouter key is kept server-side. These providers process requests under their own terms and privacy practices; routing and retention may vary. See OpenRouter's Privacy Policy.

The app uses Expo and platform services for app delivery, device features, performance monitoring and aggregated app-usage reporting. EAS Observe collects startup, render, interactive and route timings, app version, device/OS details and a pseudonymous installation ID; it may also report JavaScript errors and stack traces. Expo Insights records app cold-start events for aggregated usage reporting. Sensitive route parameters are filtered before route metrics are sent. Expo documents 90-day retention for Observe metrics, with monthly deletion processing that can leave records for up to 120 days; Insights and other provider retention can differ. See Expo's Privacy Policy. Optional PostHog analytics is off unless you enable it in Settings → Privacy. When enabled, PostHog receives pseudonymous screen names, feature events and limited metadata such as counts or categories. It does not receive email, recipe names, ingredient names, AI prompts or generated recipe text through app analytics. AI performance telemetry is similarly limited to metadata and is disabled when analytics is off. Session replay and touch autocapture are disabled. See PostHog’s Privacy Policy.

On Android, the app uses Google Mobile Ads to show ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect privacy choices where required. Google may process device or advertising identifiers, ad requests, approximate location or other device signals, and ad impressions/interactions for ad selection and measurement. Ads are requested only after UMP reports that requests are allowed. Where required, you can reopen privacy choices in Settings → Privacy. See Google's Privacy Policy.

How we use information

We use information to provide recipes, accounts, sync, recipe sharing, pantry and shopping features, cooking progress, achievements, preferences, notifications and AI assistance, and to maintain and troubleshoot the service. Recipe views and category interactions support personalization. The app code does not show that this interaction history is used to train an AI model.

Retention

Local information remains on your device until removed or overwritten. The app does not set a fixed retention period for account data, public recipes, profiles, leaderboard records, uploaded images, AI provider requests, Supabase logs, backups or push tokens. Expo currently documents 90-day retention for EAS Observe metrics, with monthly deletion processing that can leave records for up to 120 days. Insights and other provider retention can depend on provider policy and configuration. Guest data stored only on a device may be lost if app data is cleared or the app is removed.

Your choices and requests

You may use the app as a guest, choose whether to grant camera, photo and notification permissions, and edit some profile and preference data. Signing out ends the current Supabase session but does not delete local or cloud data.

The app does not currently provide self-service account deletion, full data deletion or data export. You can disable optional PostHog analytics at any time in Settings → Privacy. On Android, where Google UMP requires privacy choices, use Settings → Privacy → Manage ad privacy choices to reopen them. To make a privacy request, email praisetechzw@gmail.com. We may ask for information to verify your request and will respond under applicable law.

Security

The app uses Supabase Auth sessions and user-scoped database policies for private app-state rows. Owner-restricted policies govern profile, recipe, leaderboard and image writes. AI requests use a server-side Supabase Function so the OpenRouter key is not bundled in the mobile app. No code description can guarantee the security of hosted providers or operational practices.

Children and international processing

The app does not implement an age gate. If you are not legally able to use the app independently, use it only with permission from a parent or legal guardian. Supabase, OpenRouter/model providers and Expo may process information outside your country; the locations depend on provider configuration and AI routing.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and make the current version available in the app and on this page. Privacy questions or requests: Praise Masunga, Zimbabwe Traditional Recipes, praisetechzw@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current app implementation. Review it against live provider settings and applicable law before release.
