## Suggested full description

Bring the flavours of Zimbabwe into your kitchen. Zimbabwe Traditional Recipes helps you discover traditional dishes, plan meals around ingredients you already have, and follow recipes one step at a time.

**Discover and cook**  
Browse Zimbabwean recipes, search for something to make, and follow clear ingredient lists and cooking steps.

**Start with your pantry**  
Choose ingredients you have available and explore recipe ideas that fit your kitchen.

**Make it personal**  
Choose English, ChiShona or isiNdebele, set up your chef profile, save favourites and keep a shopping list.

**Ask the AI Chef**  
Get AI-assisted ingredient recognition and recipe suggestions. AI output can be inaccurate or incomplete, so check ingredients, quantities and food-safety details before cooking.

Use guest mode to keep progress on your device, or create an account to sync selected app data. Account and AI features require an internet connection.

Created by PraiseTechzw.
